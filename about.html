About Formify 3D

Hi, I'm Yusuf — the creator behind Formify 3D. I design and print creative 3D models including fidgets, custom decorations, household helpers and fun articulated prints.

I started Formify 3D to turn ideas into real products while learning design, business and creativity through 3D printing.

Every print is carefully made using quality PLA filament and tested to make sure it looks great and feels satisfying to use.
